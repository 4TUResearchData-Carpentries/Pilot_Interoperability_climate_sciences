# Pilot Debrief Session

## Pilot Workshop Debrief

After you have completed your lesson pilot, participate in one of the regularly scheduled Pilot Workshop Debrief community sessions hosted by The Carpentries. The purpose of these events is to give members of The Carpentries lesson development community an opportunity to report on their experiences teaching lessons under development, to share their perspectives, and to exchange knowledge and advice.

- Sign up on the Community Sessions Etherpad.
- Double-check the time zone before confirming your attendance.
- Mark your calendar after signing up.
- At the time of the event, the Zoom link can be found at the top of the Etherpad.


**During the pilot**, it is helpful to take notes to answer the following questions:

## Questions to capture during the pilot

- How long did it take to teach the episode?
- How long did learners need to complete each exercise?
- What questions did learners have?
- What technical difficulties were encountered?
- What parts of the lesson appeared to be confusing for learners?

## Learner feedback

At the end of the pilot, make sure to collect feedback from the learners. You can use the methods described in "Preparing to teach" to gather feedback, for example:

- Minute cards
- One up, one down
- Ask learners about the exercises in your episode

## Reflection after the pilot

As soon as possible after the pilot has finished — ideally immediately afterwards — take time to reflect on the following questions, based on both the feedback collected and your own perspective as the instructor and author of the lesson content. Make notes on your answers to these questions. You will return to these notes during the Pilot Workshop Debrief session.

- What worked well, both in terms of content and delivery?
- What did not work as well?
- How close were your time estimates to the actual time needed to teach the material and complete the exercises?
- How did the audience perceive the difficulty level of the material?
- What will you do differently next time?
- What will you change in the material you taught?
- What will you change in the way you collect feedback in future pilots?

## Final step

When you have completed the lesson pilots and made these notes, send them to curriculum@carpentries.org.
